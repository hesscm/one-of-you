## Fixed: the log encoding defect the 19:00 session deferred

into a UTF-8 log, so the one line recording success was the one a later
session could not grep. It also wraps a native command's stderr in a
NativeCommandError block. Replaced both redirects with an explicit
UTF-8 pipeline through `ToString()`; `$LASTEXITCODE` still reports the
exe's status through the pipeline (verified). Parse-checked: 603 tokens.

## Addendum — trigger moved to 20:00, and my recommendation was wrong

Chris said "sure, 1 hour later." Set: StartBoundary 20:00 local,
NextRunTime 2026-09-05 20:00, StartWhenAvailable preserved, settle logic
re-verified against the new boundary (reads 20:00, on-time fire measures
0 min late, no settle).

But the reason I gave him for the move does not survive the arithmetic,
and I only checked after making the change:

  local 18:00 -> 23:00 UTC  (hour 23 of that UTC day)
  local 19:00 -> 00:00 UTC  (hour 0  — the rollover itself)
  local 20:00 -> 01:00 UTC  (hour 1)

The quota rolls at 00:00 UTC = 19:00 local. So moving from 19:00 to
20:00 shifts the wake from the first minute of the fresh UTC day to the
first hour of it. The unattended session still gets a nearly-full
allowance; the only budget it now inherits is whatever an attended
session happened to spend in that single hour. I told Chris "an hour or
two later" would make the unattended self inherit the day's remaining
budget. It does not. Later is the wrong direction.

What actually achieves it is **earlier**: 18:00 local = 23:00 UTC puts
the wake in the LAST hour of the UTC day, after attended sessions have
had the whole day to spend. That is the change that matches the stated
principle — the unsupervised self should have less rope than the
supervised one, not more.

Left at 20:00 because that is what he asked for and it is not harmful,
only insufficient. Flagged to him with the numbers.
